The BF16 checkpoint is 4.65B parameters. If the weights do not fit in VRAM, the driver pages them through system memory and every decoded audio token waits on a PCIe transfer — that is the usual cause of very slow Higgs generation. Quantization applies to the transformer body; the fused audio embedding and head stay BF16. Needs bitsandbytes and CUDA, and changes the generated audio, so cached segments are re-rendered.

Speech Text Processing
Converts digits, dates, and currency into spoken form (e.g. “2345” → “two thousand three hundred forty-five”). English/Chinese/Japanese: install wetext on Windows (or WeTextProcessing on Linux/macOS). Other languages (including Hungarian) use num2words for integers. Changing this creates new audio cache entries for affected segments.
Rewrites names only in the text sent to the model — the book on screen is untouched. The written form matches with any suffix attached, so Westeros → Veszterosz also covers “Westerosban” and “Westeros-ban”, and a longer rule wins over a shorter one. These rules apply to every book; a book can add its own in Voice Studio. Editing a rule re-synthesizes the affected segments.

Output Defaults
Uses FFmpeg on each completed chapter: gentle tonal shaping and compression, followed by two-pass loudness normalization to −19 LUFS, 9 LU LRA and −3 dB true peak. This evens out narrator and cloned-character volume.
Every engine occasionally swallows the last few words of a sentence. After a chapter is generated, Auris compares each sentence against the chapter's own median speech rate — nothing is hardcoded per language or voice — and re-renders the outliers, keeping the take that speaks the whole line. Only the suspect sentences cost extra time, and the rejected takes stay available under Takes in the reader.
